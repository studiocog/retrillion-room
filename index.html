About

I'm curious by default. I like taking an idea from one field and seeing what it does in another: design next to engineering, systems next to stories, tools next to the people who use them.

Most of what I make starts as a question. How does this work? What would it look like if it were built differently? Then I build something to find out.

This room is one of those experiments. Pick a tape to see what else I've been working on.
